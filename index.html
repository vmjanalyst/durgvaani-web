How to Reach the Fort

🚡 Raigad Ropeway

The Raigad ropeway is the fastest way to reach the summit. The base station is located near Pachad village. Ropeway timings are typically from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM daily. The scenic ride takes just 5-7 minutes, bypassing the steep climb while offering breathtaking views of the Sahyadri mountains.

🥾 Raigad Trek Start Point

For adventure enthusiasts, the traditional Raigad trek start point begins near Pachad village, leading you through the historic Chit Darwaja and the grand Maha Darwaja. The trek consists of approximately 1,500 stone steps and takes most trekkers about 1.5 to 2 hours to ascend.
